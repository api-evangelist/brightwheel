Quick answer: Inquiry-based learning is a teaching approach in which children build understanding by asking questions, investigating, and explaining what they find, while the teacher guides the process instead of simply delivering answers. In a preschool classroom, that might mean exploring what a seed needs to grow. Teachers can choose from four levels of inquiry, from confirmation to open inquiry, depending on how much structure children need.
