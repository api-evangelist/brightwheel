Which literacy activities work best for preschoolers? The best literacy activities for preschoolers are short, playful, and focused on one skill at a time. Use letter games for letter recognition, rhymes and songs for phonological awareness, story retelling for vocabulary and narrative skills, playdough and drawing for early writing, and shared reading for print awareness.
